Labcin Jump

Labcin runs through the lab. Jump over the bugs and collect the code bubbles: each one is worth 10 points.

Score 0Best 0

How to play

  • Jump with Space, ↑ or by tapping the screen. Hold it down to jump higher.
  • Jump over the bugs on the floor. Dodge the high flyers by staying down.
  • Pause with P or Esc.
