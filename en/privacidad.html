Privacy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The idea behind Pache Labs is simple: your files are yours. This is what the programs do and don't do, starting with Ozone PDF.

Ozone PDF collects no data

  • It does not connect to the internet. It does not send your documents, photos or any other data to any server, mine or anyone else's.
  • It has no ads, accounts or usage statistics. I don't know who uses it or what for.
  • Everything is processed on your computer. The files you create are saved wherever you choose. If that folder syncs with a cloud service (such as OneDrive or Google Drive), that service does upload them, under its own policy.

What it stores on your computer

  • Preferences: the theme (light or dark), the language, the "Open the result when done" checkbox and whether Labcin is on. They are saved in the program's data folder, inside your user account.
  • Your signature: only if you choose to save it to use again, it stays in that same folder. You can delete it from the signing window.
  • Camera photos: if you use "Photos to PDF", the photos stay in the program's memory while you work and are discarded when you finish or close it.

The camera

Ozone PDF uses the camera only when you open "Photos to PDF" and only while that window is open. The images never leave your computer. You can allow or block access in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Camera (on Mac, in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera).

This site

This site uses no cookies, tracking or advertising tools, and the fonts are loaded from the site itself. The service that hosts it may record technical data about visits (such as the IP address) for security, under its own policy. If you make a donation, the platform you choose (Cafecito or GitHub Sponsors) handles your payment data; I only see what that platform shows me.

Suggestion form

If you send me a suggestion from this site, the message (and your name and email, if you write them) is sent through Web3Forms, a form delivery service, and reaches me by email. I only use it to read and reply to you; I don't share it or use it for advertising. If you want me to delete a message of yours, ask me by email.
