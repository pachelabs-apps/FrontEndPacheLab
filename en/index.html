Why offline

Cyberattacks keep growing, and artificial intelligence makes finding and exploiting flaws faster and easier than ever. When you upload a document to a website, it is no longer in your hands: it can be stored on a server, leak in a hack or be used in ways you never chose.

That is why I make tools that work offline. If your sensitive data (an ID, a payslip, a contract) never leaves your computer, it is not exposed anywhere.
