Catch your data

Your data is falling: ID, passwords, photos and more. Catch it with Labcin before it hits the floor and dodge the viruses. Every mistake gives you a privacy tip.

Data 0Best 0Level 1❤️❤️❤️

How to play

  • Move Labcin with the mouse, by dragging your finger or with ← →.
  • Catch 🪪 🔑 📷 💳 📍 ✉️ and dodge 🦠 🎣 🕵️. If you drop a piece of data or catch a virus, you lose a life.
  • Every 10 pieces of data you level up and everything falls faster. Pause with P or Esc.

In real life, the best way to protect your data is not to upload it just anywhere. That's why Ozone PDF works without internet.
